### What I Built

**[NyayaVeda AI](https://www.nyayaveda.co.in)** — India's High-Precision Legal AI.

Not a wrapper. Not a chatbot pointed at legal PDFs.  
A purpose-built legal reasoning engine, trained and validated specifically for Indian law.

**Corpus — measured, not estimated:**
- 34,293,080 judgment chunks — Supreme Court + all 25 High Courts
- 1,159,915 statutory provisions — 858 Central Acts + all state laws
- BNS / BNSS / BSA with 1,233-pair IPC→BNS concordance
- Coverage: 1953 to October 2026

**What it does that others don't:**
- Enforces binding authority by design — cannot return a HC judgment as binding over a SC ruling
- Full Hindi support — question in Devanagari, answer with citations in Hindi
- Automatic IPC → BNS cross-referencing for the new criminal codes
- Bench analytics — judge-level citation patterns for courtroom preparation

**Built for the district court advocate — not just elite law firms.**
